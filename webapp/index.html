NARUTO DRAFT

The Ultimate Shinobi Team-Building Card Game

How it works

  • 200-character database, drawn at random — no duplicates.
  • Fill all 6 roles: Leader, Attacker, Defender, Support, Healer, Wildcard.
  • You get 2 discards per match. After that, every roll must be drafted.
  • Highest role chemistry + OVR wins the simulated battle.
